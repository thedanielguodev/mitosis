Mitosis: Cell Division

Learning goal: by the end, you should be able to name each stage of mitosis in order, describe what happens to the DNA and the cell membrane at each one, and explain why the cell needs mitochondria to pull it off.

Step through the 5 phases with the buttons at the bottom of the screen. Click on parts of the cell (chromosomes, membranes, spindle fibers, mitochondria) to learn what they do. Move around with the pad in the top-right corner, and look around by dragging.

Division complete

One cell became two, each with a full, identical copy of the DNA — that's mitosis. Your body runs this process constantly to grow, replace worn-out cells, and heal wounds, and every step of it is powered by the ATP the mitochondria supplied along the way.
